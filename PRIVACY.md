# Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026.

## Data stays on your Mac

Power Watcher does not collect, upload, sell, or share data from the app. It has
no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking SDKs, or background network requests.

It reads current battery capacity and power-source state to decide when to show
a warning, and display geometry and Reduce Motion to display it correctly.
These readings are used in memory, not retained as a history or sent anywhere.

## Local settings

The app stores monitoring, steady-glow, and first-launch preferences locally.
macOS manages the optional login item. These settings remain until changed or
removed; the maintainer holds no copy.

You can pause monitoring, disable Open at login, change settings, or quit at any
time. Uninstalling may leave local preferences. After quitting, you can remove
the `com.kovyrin.PowerWatcher` preference domain; sandboxed builds store it inside
the matching app container in `~/Library/Containers/`. Disable the login item
before uninstalling.

The opt-in developer self-check uses temporary settings and writes images only
to its chosen local artifact directory. Temporary settings are removed after
normal completion. You can delete the artifacts at any time.

## External links and support

Project and privacy links open in your browser. GitHub and other websites apply
their own policies. Issues are public; do not upload private screenshots,
credentials, or full system logs.

Contact the maintainer through the
[project repository](https://github.com/kovyrin/power-watcher) with policy
questions. There is no app-collected server data to request or delete.
This policy will be updated if data handling changes.
