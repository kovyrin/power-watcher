# Code of Conduct

Treat everyone with respect. Give constructive feedback, assume good intent, and
keep discussions focused on the project. People of all backgrounds and experience
levels are welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable. These expectations apply to
issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to
keep the community welcoming, considering context and responding proportionately.

For private conduct concerns, contact [@kovyrin](https://github.com/kovyrin) through
a contact method on their profile. Do not post private reports in public issues.
