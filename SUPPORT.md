# Support

Use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/kovyrin/power-watcher/issues) for bugs,
questions, and feature requests. Include macOS/app versions, Mac architecture,
and relevant display/Spaces setup. Keep screenshots privacy-safe. Report
vulnerabilities through [Security](SECURITY.md).

## Common questions

### Where is the app?

Power Watcher lives in the menu bar with a shield-and-bolt icon and no Dock icon.
Click for controls, right-click for Open and Quit, or open the app again.

### How do I test it?

Click a 3%, 2%, or 1% card for a six-second preview. The sequence runs all levels
over nine seconds. Previews can be stopped immediately.

### Why is there no glow?

Normal warnings need monitoring enabled, battery power, and a reading at 3% or
below. External power clears them even when charging is paused. Previews work
without an internal battery.

### How do I start it automatically?

Move the app to Applications, open it there, and enable Open at login. If macOS
needs approval, use the Login Items button shown in the controls. Keep the app
in the same location afterward.

### Why is the glow steady?

Use a steady glow or the macOS Reduce Motion setting is enabled. Steady mode
keeps the glow at full intensity.

### Can it prevent a shutdown?

No. Battery estimates vary, and a Mac can sleep before reporting 1%. Try the
previews in your normal workspace before relying on the warnings.
